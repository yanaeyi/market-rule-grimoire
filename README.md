# Market Rule Grimoire

> *The definitive spellbook of market logic, trading algorithms, and mathematical models.*

## Overview
**Market Rule Grimoire:** Standardized rule sets and logic configurations for the system. Basic parameter specifications, operational constraints, and condition definitions for cross-module invocation. 

## Core Objectives
- **Strategy Library:** A version-controlled, highly modular collection of algorithmic trading logics.
- **Signal Zoo:** Precise definitions and mathematical formulas for cross-sectional and time-series market signals.
- **Risk Paradigms:** Pre-trade and post-trade risk management logic, including position sizing, dynamic exposure, and capital allocation algorithms.

## Relationship with the Ecosystem
The *Grimoire* dictates **what** to trade and **when**. It is absolutely agnostic to the execution details. The models stored here are derived from the theoretical groundwork of the market-rule-lab and consumed by the market-rule-forge for real-time execution.
